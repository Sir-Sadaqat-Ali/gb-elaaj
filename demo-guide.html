💬 Anticipated Client Questions & Strongest Answers
Q1: "Is the system 100% working and ready?"
Answer: "Yes, the complete core engine (Next.js 15, PostgreSQL database, Prisma ORM, Auth.js) is 100% functional. All 7 role portals (Patient, Doctor, Admin, Pharmacy, Lab, Nurse, Finance) are fully built and running. For full public launch, we simply plug in the production credentials (Agora for live video, Mapbox for maps, and payment gateway)."
Q2: "How will doctors and patients get onto the platform when launched?"
Answer: "Patients can self-register in 30 seconds via phone/email. Doctors register via 'Join as Doctor' with their PMDC license number and degrees. Every doctor remains in 'Pending' status until Super Admin manually verifies their license and approves them."
Q3: "How does the system ensure safety for Home Nursing and Labs?"
Answer: "Every nurse profile requires Pakistan Nursing Council (PNC) registration and police character clearance. Additionally, real-time GPS tracking (Mapbox) records the exact arrival and departure times of nurses and sample-collection riders."
Q4: "Can a patient in Gilgit consult an Islamabad doctor, but get medicine locally?"
Answer: "Absolutely! Doctor consultations are digital/video (location independent), while Pharmacies and Labs are strictly hyper-local (city-filtered). Patients receive an instant PDF E-Prescription which they can either purchase from their neighborhood pharmacy or order via local city delivery."
Q5: "How does the platform make money?"
Answer: "The platform has an automated commission split engine built in (15% on consultations, 8% on pharmacy, 12% on lab tests). Every transaction automatically splits between the provider's wallet and the platform's revenue."
